About Booking Mongolia

Local Mongolia travel support built from real tourism experience.

Our Story

Booking Mongolia was created from real travel and tourism experience in Mongolia.

The founder has traveled to more than 20 countries and over 50 cities around the world, both independently and through tour companies.

Over the last 10 years, the founder has worked in Mongolia’s tourism industry as a tour guide and cook, serving international travelers from many countries.

This website was built to help travelers find simple, useful and trustworthy information about Mongolia travel, tours, destinations and local experiences.

Our Mission

Our goal is to make traveling in Mongolia easier by providing clear travel information, recommended routes, destination ideas and useful booking options.

We want more travelers to discover Mongolia’s beautiful nature, nomadic culture and unique experiences.
