Booking Mongolia is becoming an integrated travel platform that connects travelers with individual travel services and local providers, not only a fixed-package operator.

Ask for one service, or combine several. Tours, hotels, guides, and cars are listed on the site, and inquiries are welcome.

Founder, Booking Mongolia

The founder has more than 10 years of hands-on tourism work: tour planning, itinerary development, traveler assistance, and coordinating drivers, guides, and travel services.

The founder has personally traveled to more than 10 countries and over 30 cities. That experience inspired Booking Mongolia.

That background covers both the traveler’s side of a trip and the practical work of organizing services.

Why we created Booking Mongolia

Travelers need a clear way to choose services, see what is offered, and reach the people who provide them. Booking Mongolia started from that practical tourism work.
