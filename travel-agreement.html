3. Booking Process

  • Operator and Guest first confirm travel dates, itinerary, traveler count, and availability.
  • Operator sends this Travel Agreement for review.

  • Accommodations and key services may be reserved after deposit/confirmation, because popular ger camps can fill quickly.

4. Changes & Cancellation

  • Date changes requested 7 or more days before start can usually be processed without an extra change fee, subject to availability.
  • Changes requested less than 7 days before start may require an additional charge.

5. Responsibilities

  • Guest is responsible for valid passport/visa and travel insurance.
  • Operator arranges agreed local services (guide, driver, vehicle, selected accommodations) as described in the itinerary.
  • Weather, road conditions, and remote logistics in Mongolia may require flexible timing. Operator will adjust safely when needed.
  • Third-party hotels, ger camps, and activity providers remain responsible for their own service quality under their own terms.

6. Confirmation

By signing (or confirming by email/WhatsApp reply “I agree”), both sides confirm the details above. Small itinerary adjustments may still be made by mutual agreement to improve comfort and safety.
