Your Local Partner in Mongolia

Booking Mongolia is based in Mongolia and is actively developing international cooperation with agencies, operators, and organizers abroad.

We combine local destination knowledge with operations inside the country. Our work covers incoming travel for visitors coming to Mongolia and outgoing travel planning for journeys that start here. Programs include private tours and group travel.

We work with local accommodation, guides, drivers, camps, and tourism businesses, so international partners have a direct counterpart on the ground. BookingMongolia.com is our digital platform for destination information, tours, and partnership inquiries.

This page is an introduction from the Mongolian side. We want to present Mongolia clearly and be the local ground-handling partner for companies that sell or organize travel for their own clients.
