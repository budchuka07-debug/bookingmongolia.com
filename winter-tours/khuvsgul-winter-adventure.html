Khuvsgul Ice & Winter Adventure

5–7 days

Home / Winter Tours / Khuvsgul Ice & Winter Adventure

Introduction

This 5–7 day journey stays with the frozen landscapes of Khuvsgul, nomadic culture, and winter photography. A horse-drawn sleigh is used only where a local operator is working. Ice activities happen only in officially designated safe areas. A visit with a local family can be added when a household can host. Activities depend on a local safety assessment and the weather.

Highlights

  • Frozen lake landscapes around Khuvsgul
  • Horse-drawn sleigh only where a local operator is working
  • Nomadic culture and an optional visit with a local family
  • Winter photography
  • Ice activities only in officially designated safe areas

Duration and travel style

5–7 days. Private or small group. The season is November–April, and the route is planned on request.

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1 — Murun to Khatgal

Travel from Murun toward Khatgal on Khuvsgul. The summer-road estimate is about 100 km, and the drive takes longer in snow. The driver confirms the time. The evening is a heated ger or a heated room after Booking Mongolia confirms it.

Day 2 — Frozen lake landscapes

The day is for winter photography and the frozen lake landscapes near Khatgal. Any ice activity stays inside an officially designated safe area and follows the local safety assessment.

Day 3 — Sleigh and a family visit

A horse-drawn sleigh runs only where a local operator is working that day. An optional visit with a local family happens if the household can receive guests. Horseback riding on the frozen lake is not offered.

Day 4 — Nomadic culture

A slower day with nomadic culture: livestock near the ger, time with the host family, and photographs when the light is good. If the weather turns, the plan stays near the confirmed lodging.

Day 5 — What the weather allows

The group keeps the activities that are actually operating: photography, a short time on designated ice, or a quiet day. Closed activities are left out.

Day 6 — Extra day on the longer plan

Used on the 6- or 7-day version if snow slows the road or you want another day by the lake. It does not add a new list of promised activities.

Day 7 — Return toward Murun

Return toward Murun. The longer plan exists because snow and ice can change the drive. The driver confirms the winter road.

Winter clothing

Bring thermal layers, insulated trousers, winter boots, gloves, a hat, and face protection.

Winter safety

Winter temperatures can be about −20°C to −35°C depending on the place and the weather. Thermal layers, insulated trousers, winter boots, gloves, a hat, and face protection are essential. Heated accommodation is confirmed for each booking. Remote routes may change because of snow, ice, or the road. Frozen-lake activities take place only in designated areas under local instructions. Travelers should arrange their own travel insurance and emergency cover.

Activities on this tour depend on a local safety assessment and the weather. Horseback riding on the frozen lake is not part of the plan. There is a clear winter safety notice above, and it applies to every day on the ice.
