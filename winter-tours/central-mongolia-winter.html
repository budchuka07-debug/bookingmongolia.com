Central Mongolia Winter Discovery

5–6 days

Home / Winter Tours / Central Mongolia Winter Discovery

Introduction

Five or six days through central Mongolia in winter: Khugnu Khan, Elsen Tasarkhai, Kharkhorin, the Orkhon Valley, and Hustai. Every stop is checked for winter access before a booking is confirmed. Travel times are confirmed with the driver. The distances below are summer-road estimates and take longer in snow.

Highlights

  • Khugnu Khan and Elsen Tasarkhai
  • Kharkhorin and Erdene Zuu
  • Orkhon Valley winter landscapes
  • Hustai National Park on the way back
  • Camel riding and Tsenkher only when they are operating

Duration and travel style

5–6 days. Private or small group. The season is November–April, and the route is planned on request.

Summer-road estimates, longer in snow: Ulaanbaatar to Khugnu Khan / Elsen Tasarkhai is about 280 km. Ulaanbaatar to Kharkhorin is about 360 km. Ulaanbaatar to Hustai National Park is about 100 km. Travel times are confirmed with the driver. This page does not give a kilometre figure for the road from Kharkhorin to Tsenkher.

Accommodation is a confirmed heated ger or room.

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1 — Ulaanbaatar, Khugnu Khan, Elsen Tasarkhai

Drive from Ulaanbaatar toward Khugnu Khan and Elsen Tasarkhai, about 280 km on a summer-road estimate. Snow makes the day longer. The driver confirms the time, and the stop is used only if winter access is open.

Day 2 — Camels and a family visit

Camel riding only if the weather and the operator allow it, plus a visit with a nomadic family when a household can host.

Day 3 — Kharkhorin and Erdene Zuu

Kharkhorin and Erdene Zuu. Ulaanbaatar to Kharkhorin is about 360 km as a summer-road estimate. The driver confirms how long the day's road takes, and the stop is checked for winter access.

Day 4 — Orkhon Valley

Orkhon Valley winter landscapes, staying on roads that are open that day.

Day 5 — Tsenkher only if it is open

Tsenkher Hot Springs only if the spring is operating and the road is open. This day runs only in that case, and the driver confirms the time. There is no published kilometre figure for the Kharkhorin to Tsenkher road.

Day 6 — Hustai, then Ulaanbaatar

Hustai National Park, then Ulaanbaatar. Ulaanbaatar to Hustai is about 100 km on a summer-road estimate, and the drive takes longer in snow. The driver confirms the time.

Winter clothing

Bring thermal layers, insulated trousers, winter boots, gloves, a hat, and face protection.

Winter safety

Winter temperatures can be about −20°C to −35°C depending on the place and the weather. Thermal layers, insulated trousers, winter boots, gloves, a hat, and face protection are essential. Heated accommodation is confirmed for each booking. Remote routes may change because of snow, ice, or the road. Frozen-lake activities take place only in designated areas under local instructions. Travelers should arrange their own travel insurance and emergency cover.
