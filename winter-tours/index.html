Winter Mongolia Tours – Discover the Magic of Mongolia in Winter.

Experience Mongolia's spectacular winter landscapes, nomadic culture, frozen lakes, historic destinations and unique outdoor adventures.

Winter Mongolia

Khuvsgul Ice & Winter Adventure

Duration: 5–7 days

Frozen lake landscapes, winter photography, and nomadic culture around Khuvsgul. Ice activities stay in officially designated safe areas.

Main activities

Horse-drawn sleigh only where a local operator is working, winter photography, an optional visit with a local family, and ice activities only in designated safe areas.

Khentii – In the Footsteps of Chinggis Khan

Duration: 3–4 days

Ulaanbaatar, the Chinggis Khan Equestrian Statue at Tsonjin Boldog, and Khentii landscapes when the winter road allows.

Main activities

The statue complex, local history at stops that are open, and Dadal with Deluun Boldog only if the road and access allow.

Central Mongolia Winter Discovery

Duration: 5–6 days

Khugnu Khan, Elsen Tasarkhai, Kharkhorin, the Orkhon Valley, and Hustai. Every stop is checked for winter access before a booking is confirmed.

Main activities

Erdene Zuu, winter landscapes, camel riding only if the weather and the operator allow it, and Tsenkher Hot Springs only if the spring and the road are open.

Terelj Winter Experience

Duration: 1–2 days

A short winter trip from Ulaanbaatar into Gorkhi-Terelj. Each activity depends on the weather and the local operator.

Main activities

Chinggis Khan statue complex, Turtle Rock (Melkhii Khad), dog sledding, camel riding, horseback riding, a traditional ger stay, winter photography, and an optional cultural visit. They do not all run on the same day.
