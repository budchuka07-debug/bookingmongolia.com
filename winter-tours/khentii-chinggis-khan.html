Khentii – In the Footsteps of Chinggis Khan

3–4 days

Home / Winter Tours / Khentii – In the Footsteps of Chinggis Khan

Introduction

A 3–4 day private or small-group journey from Ulaanbaatar toward Khentii, visiting places linked with Chinggis Khan where the winter road and local access allow. The trip is planned on request for November–April.

Highlights

  • Chinggis Khan Equestrian Statue at Tsonjin Boldog
  • Khentii landscapes and local history at stops that are open
  • Dadal and Deluun Boldog only if the road and access allow
  • A return to Ulaanbaatar, with an optional fourth day

Duration and travel style

3–4 days. Private or small group. The season is November–April, and the route is planned on request.

Burkhan Khaldun and remote Khentii sites need special planning, access permission, and a winter road check. They are not guaranteed stops.

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1 — Ulaanbaatar and Tsonjin Boldog

The day starts in Ulaanbaatar, visits Tsonjin Boldog and the Chinggis Khan Equestrian Statue, and continues toward Khentii only if the road allows. Ulaanbaatar to Tsonjin Boldog is about 54 km on a summer-road estimate and takes longer in snow. The driver confirms the time.

Day 2 — Khentii landscapes

Khentii landscapes and local history, using only the stops that are accessible that day.

Day 3 — Dadal only if access allows

Dadal and Deluun Boldog only if the road and access allow, then the return begins. If access is closed, the group does not force the extra drive.

Day 4 — Optional

Optional extra sightseeing, or the return to Ulaanbaatar. A three-day plan returns on day 3.

Winter clothing

Bring thermal layers, insulated trousers, winter boots, gloves, a hat, and face protection.

Winter safety

Winter temperatures can be about −20°C to −35°C depending on the place and the weather. Thermal layers, insulated trousers, winter boots, gloves, a hat, and face protection are essential. Heated accommodation is confirmed for each booking. Remote routes may change because of snow, ice, or the road. Frozen-lake activities take place only in designated areas under local instructions. Travelers should arrange their own travel insurance and emergency cover.

Burkhan Khaldun and remote Khentii sites need special planning, access permission, and a winter road check. They are not guaranteed stops.
