Winter Snow Trekking and Hiking

A guided snow walk of one or two days in Terelj or a nearby valley. The route stays on paths the guide accepts that day. Wind, ice, and poor visibility stop the hike.

Itinerary estimate

These lengths are planning estimates inside the November–April season. They are not a confirmed departure date. Exact festival days, where a tour follows one, are confirmed on the quote.

1 Day

  1. Morning: Pickup and a short drive to the trailhead.
  2. Midday: Guided walk with photo stops.
  3. Afternoon: Return to Ulaanbaatar before dark.

2 Days

  1. Day 1: Walk and overnight in a heated ger near the valley.
  2. Day 2: Second short route and return to the city.

Weather and safety

Wear boots with grip, warm layers, and sunglasses. The guide turns back if the path is icy, the wind rises, or daylight is too short. This is not a glacier or high-alpine climb.
