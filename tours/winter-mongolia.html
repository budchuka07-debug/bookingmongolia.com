North · usually 4 days

Tsaatan reindeer trip

A winter visit to Dukha reindeer herders in the northern taiga. The road is cold and slow. The group stays small. You see the herd, the winter camp, and how the family moves with the animals.

About $250 / person / day

Central · usually 3 days

Orkhon ice festival

Winter days in the Orkhon valley when the river and the ground are frozen. Ice games, a valley walk, and a night in a warm ger. Festival dates are confirmed when you book.

About $250 / person / day

Umnugovi · usually early March

Umnugovi camel festival

The camel festival in South Gobi: camel racing, herder dress, and the open desert in late winter. Often held around early March. The exact days follow that year’s festival.

About $250 / person / day

Near the city · 1–2 days

Terelj dog sled

A short winter day in Terelj National Park. Dog sled on the snow, frozen streams, and the rock valleys close to Ulaanbaatar. Easy to add before a longer festival trip.

About $250 / person / day

Countryside · usually 2–3 days

Herder family in winter

Stay with a herder family and see winter work: animals brought close to the ger, frozen milk, felt, and the evening fire. This is a culture visit, not a show.

About $250 / person / day

Khuvsgul · late winter

Khuvsgul ice festival

The ice festival on frozen Khuvsgul Lake: sleds, ice shapes, and winter games on the Blue Pearl. Usually in late winter. See the longer ice-festival page for the lake route.

About $250 / person / day

How a winter quote works

Pick the festivals and the family stay, then add the days. One person for one day is about $250. Two people for three days is about $1,500. The day rate is a guide for winter travel on the ground: guide, the day’s transport, a simple warm stay, and the activity named above. Domestic flights, festival tickets if a town charges one, and drinks are written separately on the quote.
