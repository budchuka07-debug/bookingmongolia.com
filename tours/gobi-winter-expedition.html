5–7 Days · November – April

Gobi Winter Expedition

Five to seven days in the South Gobi in winter: Flaming Cliffs, Khongoryn Els, Yolyn Am, and camel herder families where camps are open. The road order changes with weather.

Itinerary estimate

These lengths are planning estimates inside the November–April season. They are not a confirmed departure date. Exact festival days, where a tour follows one, are confirmed on the quote.

5 Days

  1. Day 1: Fly or drive to Dalanzadgad and Yolyn Am.
  2. Day 2: Bayanzag, the Flaming Cliffs.
  3. Day 3: Khongoryn Els.
  4. Day 4: Camel family or a dune morning.
  5. Day 5: Return to Ulaanbaatar.

6 Days

  1. Days 1–4: Same Gobi sites at a slower pace.
  2. Day 5: Spare driving day.
  3. Day 6: Return.

7 Days

  1. Days 1–5: The 5-day route plus one camp day held for wind or a closed track.
  2. Days 6–7: Return to Ulaanbaatar.
