8–10 Days · November – April

Western Mongolia Eagle Hunter Experience

Eight to ten days in Bayan-Ulgii with Kazakh eagle-hunter families. The visit covers winter landscapes, photography, and home hospitality. A hunt itself is never guaranteed.

Itinerary estimate

These lengths are planning estimates inside the November–April season. They are not a confirmed departure date. Exact festival days, where a tour follows one, are confirmed on the quote.

8 Days

  1. Day 1: Fly west from Ulaanbaatar.
  2. Day 2: Drive to the host valley.
  3. Days 3–6: Family stay, eagles, and winter landscape days.
  4. Days 7–8: Return flight to Ulaanbaatar.

9 Days

  1. Days 1–6: Same as the 8-day plan.
  2. Day 7: Extra valley or a second family.
  3. Days 8–9: Return to Ulaanbaatar.

10 Days

  1. Days 1–7: Same as the 9-day plan, with one weather day held in reserve.
  2. Days 8–10: Return west road and flight.
