Winter Horse Riding Experience

Two or three days of winter horse riding where the ground and the horses are fit for it. Nights are in a ger. The ride is shortened or cancelled if the track is icy.

Itinerary estimate

These lengths are planning estimates inside the November–April season. They are not a confirmed departure date. Exact festival days, where a tour follows one, are confirmed on the quote.

2 Days

  1. Day 1: Leave Ulaanbaatar, ride, and overnight in a ger.
  2. Day 2: Morning ride and return to the city.

3 Days

  1. Day 1: Leave the city and settle at the ger.
  2. Day 2: Full riding day.
  3. Day 3: Short ride and return to Ulaanbaatar.
