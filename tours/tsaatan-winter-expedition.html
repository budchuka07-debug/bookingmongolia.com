Tsaatan Reindeer Expedition

A winter expedition to a Tsaatan reindeer-herding community in the northern taiga. The length is planned as 7, 8, or 9 days. A teepee stay and reindeer riding depend on the host family and the herd.

Itinerary estimate

These lengths are planning estimates inside the November–April season. They are not a confirmed departure date. Exact festival days, where a tour follows one, are confirmed on the quote.

7 Days

  1. Day 1: Ulaanbaatar briefing and overnight.
  2. Day 2: Fly north and drive toward the taiga road.
  3. Day 3: Reach the host community.
  4. Day 4: Reindeer herding and camp life.
  5. Day 5: Photography, and a ride only if the herd allows it.
  6. Day 6: Leave the taiga.
  7. Day 7: Return to Ulaanbaatar.

8 Days

  1. Days 1–3: Same approach as the 7-day plan.
  2. Days 4–6: Two full days with the herd, plus a slower photography day.
  3. Days 7–8: Return north road and flight to Ulaanbaatar.

9 Days

  1. Days 1–3: Same approach as the 7-day plan.
  2. Days 4–7: Extra taiga day held for weather, a second camp, or a rest day.
  3. Days 8–9: Return to Ulaanbaatar.
