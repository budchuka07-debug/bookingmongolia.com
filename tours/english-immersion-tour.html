English Immersion in the Mongolian Wild

Seven days in English through Khuvsgul Lake, Terelj and Tsonjin Boldog. You can sit both the English class and the horse-riding lesson. A small group can stay in a herder family’s camp instead.

About this package

This is a study trip in the landscape, not a city classroom. From the first morning until the last evening the group speaks English only. You may join the English lesson and the horse-riding lesson on the same day. The two classes do not replace each other.

Duration7 days

Two ways to stay

  • Scenic route: Tsonjin Boldog, Terelj’s rock valleys, then Khuvsgul Lake.
  • Small group: a herder family camp in the countryside, with the same English class and the same riding lessons.
  • Groups of about 6–10 use the scenic camps. A pair or a family can ask for the herder stay.

English class and horse class

  • English class: speaking, listening, and a short note, on the shore or in the ger.
  • Horse-riding class: matching a horse, seat, reins, and a guided ride. Instructions are in English.
  • You can sit both. Beginners start with a short ride and a slower English track.
  • Meals with the group stay in English. A local visit is explained in English by the teacher.

Sample itinerary

Day 1
Arrive in Ulaanbaatar. Evening circle sets the English-only rule.
Day 2
Tsonjin Boldog and the Chinggis statue, then Terelj. English class in camp. First look at the horses.
Day 3
Terelj. Morning English lesson and a horse-riding lesson in the valley. Both are open to everyone.
Day 4
Travel north toward Khuvsgul. The group keeps speaking English on the road.
Day 5
Khuvsgul shore. English class by the lake, then a riding lesson or a boat when the season allows.
Day 6
Second ride and a short student talk in English about one place they saw.
Day 7
Return to Ulaanbaatar. A short spoken review closes the week.

Small-group herder camp

If the group is small, the same seven days can be based at a herder family’s camp in the countryside instead of the larger scenic camps. You still join the English class and the horse-riding lessons. The family visit is explained in English. Ask for this version when you book.

Travel tips

  • Bring layers, walking shoes, and a small notebook.
  • Weather changes quickly between steppe, forest and the lake.
  • The week is built for a group. A private pair can ask for a slower version.
  • June to September is the clear season for the lake and the steppe camp.
