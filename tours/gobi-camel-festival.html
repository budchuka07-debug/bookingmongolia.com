Gobi Camel Festival Tour

A 4- or 5-day Gobi trip built around the camel festival: Bactrian camels, races, riding, herder families, and desert light. The exact festival days are confirmed when you book. They are not printed as a fixed calendar date.

Itinerary estimate

These lengths are planning estimates inside the November–April season. They are not a confirmed departure date. Exact festival days, where a tour follows one, are confirmed on the quote.

4 Days

  1. Day 1: Fly or drive to Dalanzadgad and continue to the camel area.
  2. Day 2: Camel herder family, riding, and the festival grounds when the event is on.
  3. Day 3: Races, performances, or a herder day if the public program is another week.
  4. Day 4: Desert scenery and return to Ulaanbaatar.

5 Days

  1. Days 1–3: Same as the 4-day plan.
  2. Day 4: Extra Gobi landscape day.
  3. Day 5: Return to Ulaanbaatar.
