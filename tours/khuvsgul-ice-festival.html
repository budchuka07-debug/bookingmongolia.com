5–7 Days · November – April

Khuvsgul Ice Festival Tour

Five to seven days at Khuvsgul for the ice festival, the frozen lake, winter photography, and local culture. Horse sledding is included only where it is actually operating. The exact festival days are confirmed before the trip is booked.

Itinerary estimate

These lengths are planning estimates inside the November–April season. They are not a confirmed departure date. Exact festival days, where a tour follows one, are confirmed on the quote.

5 Days

  1. Day 1: Fly to Murun and drive to the lake.
  2. Day 2: Frozen shore and festival grounds when the event is on.
  3. Day 3: Ice activities that are open that week, and photography.
  4. Day 4: Local culture and a second lake day.
  5. Day 5: Return to Ulaanbaatar.

6 Days

  1. Days 1–4: Same as the 5-day plan, with a slower lake day.
  2. Day 5: Spare day for weather.
  3. Day 6: Return to Ulaanbaatar.

7 Days

  1. Days 1–5: Lake, festival, and one extra northern community or rest day.
  2. Days 6–7: Return via Murun to Ulaanbaatar.
